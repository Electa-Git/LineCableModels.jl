LineCableModels

LineCableModels.LineCableModels — Module
LineCableModels

Compute the series impedances and shunt admittances of cables per unit length. The data model declares the parts of a cable, and a problem places cables above or in the earth. A formulation selects the formulas, and a backend executes the computation. The Gmsh and Measurements extensions add the finite-element backend and the propagation of input uncertainty.

LineCableModels.DataModel.flatten — Method
flatten(
    design::CableDesign
) -> LineCableModels.DataModel.CableBlueprint

Reduce a cable design to its CableBlueprint. Each terminal becomes a tube about the center of the cable. The tube spans the conductive regions of the terminal and the fills between them, from their nearest to their farthest extent. The regions outside the tube, up to the next terminal, become the layers of its dielectric interval. Regions inside the first terminal lie in no interval.

The conductive regions $i$ of a terminal carry direct current in parallel. A region of resistivity $\rho_i$, cross-section $A_i$ and overlength $\ell_i$ along its helices has the conductance $G_i = A_i / (\rho_i \ell_i)$ per unit length. Its share of the current is $w_i = G_i / \sum_j G_j$. The terminal has the resistance $R = 1 / \sum_i G_i$ and the temperature coefficient $\alpha = \sum_i w_i \alpha_i$. Its turns per unit length are $N = \sum_i w_i N_i$, where $N_i$ sums the inverse pitches of the helices of region $i$, signed by their direction. Its geometric mean radius $g$ follows from

\[\log g = \sum_i w_i^2 \log g_i + 2 \sum_{i < j} w_i w_j \log d_{ij}.\]

An annulus about the center of inner radius $a$ and outer radius $b$ has the self term

\[\log g_i = \log b - \mu_i \left[\frac{a^4}{(b^2 - a^2)^2} \log\frac{b}{a} - \frac{3a^2 - b^2}{4 (b^2 - a^2)}\right],\]

which is $\log b - \mu_i / 4$ for a circle and $\log b$ for a thin shell, $a = b$. Any other region has the self term $\log \sqrt{A_i / \pi} - \mu_i / 4$ of the circle of its area. The mutual distance $d_{ij}$ is the distance between the centroids. For a region inside the hole of an annulus, it is the mean distance of Rosa (1908), Eq. (57):

\[\log d_{ij} = \frac{b^2 \log b - a^2 \log a}{b^2 - a^2} - \frac{1}{2}.\]

The tube of inner radius $r_i$ and outer radius $r_e$ takes the resistivity $R \pi (r_e^2 - r_i^2)$. Its relative permeability gives the self term of an annulus the value $\log g$:

\[\mu_\mathrm{tube} = \frac{\log r_e - \log g} {\frac{r_i^4}{(r_e^2 - r_i^2)^2} \log\frac{r_e}{r_i} - \frac{3 r_i^2 - r_e^2}{4 (r_e^2 - r_i^2)}},\]

with the first term of the denominator zero for $r_i = 0$. The interval of the tube reaches from $r_e$ to $r_d$, and its layer $k$ of relative permeability $\mu_k$ reaches from $a_k$ to $b_k$. The interval has

\[\mu_{ins} = \frac{\sum_k \mu_k \log(b_k / a_k)}{\log(r_d / r_e)} \left[1 + \frac{2 \pi^2 N^2 (r_d^2 - r_e^2)}{\log(r_d / r_e)}\right],\]

and $\mu_{ins} = 1$ without layers.

Returns

  • A CableBlueprint with one concentric assembly, the cable.

Errors

  • Throws ArgumentError when a terminal lies in a copy of a repeat, or when a fill surrounds the regions of several terminals or of none.
  • Throws ArgumentError when the regions of a terminal are not contiguous from the inside out or do not tile an annulus about the center, or when a dielectric layer is not such an annulus.
  • Throws ArgumentError when the conductive regions of one terminal have different reference temperatures.
