Contents

  • Matrix formulation gives the transmission-line equations and the block assembly of the conductor matrices, with the reductions and the scan over the frequencies.
  • Conductors gives the internal impedance of a tubular conductor and the resistivity at the operating temperature.
  • Dielectrics gives the magnetic series term of a dielectric interval and the admittivity of insulators and semicons.
  • Earth return gives the earth-return impedance and potential coefficients of cables in two half-spaces, and the properties of the air and the earth.
