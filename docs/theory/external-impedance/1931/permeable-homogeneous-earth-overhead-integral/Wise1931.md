**Description.** Carson-type self and mutual ground-return impedance integrals with the earth's relative permeability retained instead of set to unity.

**Assumptions.**

**Expression.** Wise prints the self impedance as

```math
\begin{aligned}
K+jX&=Z=z+j2\omega\log\frac{\rho''}{a}
+j4\omega\mu\int_0^\infty
\frac{e^{-2h'\tau}}{\sqrt{\tau^2+j}+\mu\tau} \\
d\tau&=z+j2\omega\log\frac{\rho''}{a}+4\omega(P+jQ),
\end{aligned}\qquad\text{(23)}
```

and the mutual impedance as

```math
\begin{aligned}
Z_{12}&=j2\omega\log\frac{\rho''}{\rho'}
+j4\omega\mu\int_0^\infty
\frac{e^{-(h_1'+h_2')\tau}}{\sqrt{\tau^2+j}+\mu\tau}
\cos(x'\tau) \\
d\tau&=j2\omega\log\frac{\rho''}{\rho'}+4\omega(P+jQ).
\end{aligned}\qquad\text{(24)}
```

The corresponding redefinition printed immediately below (24) is

```math
P+jQ=j\mu\int_0^\infty
\frac{e^{-(h'+y')\tau}}{\sqrt{\tau^2+j}+\mu\tau}
\cos(x'\tau)\,d\tau.
```

**Approximation.** None in the displayed integral beyond the inherited Carson model. Wise separately derives asymptotic and convergent series; those lengthy evaluator series are not silently substituted for (23)–(24).

**Limitations.** The formulas use the paper's 1931 electromagnetic-unit normalization, primed coordinates, and logarithmic distance definitions. This record does not convert them to SI or repair notation using later sources. It also does not claim full-wave displacement-current validity.

**Reference.** Wise (1931), “Effect of Ground Permeability on Ground Return Circuits,” *Bell System Technical Journal* 10, 472–484. [Wise1931](@cite)

**Transcription source.** Original publication page images. The factors ``j2\omega`` and ``j4\omega\mu``, exponential arguments, denominator ``\sqrt{\tau^2+j}+\mu\tau``, cosine factor, and the separate ``4\omega(P+jQ)`` forms were visually checked on printed p. 474.

## Source transcription

Wise says the derivation follows Carson's paper while restoring every permeability factor that Carson had set to unity. Equations (23) and (24) are explicitly identified as the new infinite-integral self and mutual formulas; the following pages give permeability-dependent asymptotic and convergent evaluators.

## Evidence and approximation sources

The equation is an original-source extension of Carson's kernel, not a secondary restatement. The later series on pp. 474–479 are evaluator forms generated from the displayed integral and are therefore not counted as separate physical kernels here.

## Limitations and discrepancies

- Optical text extraction loses several primes, radicals, and Greek symbols; the printed page image controls the transcription.
- The source uses an older electromagnetic-unit system. Because the required definitions are not safely reducible to SI without a derivation, the record preserves the printed normalization and labels its units accordingly.
- Historical priority remains Carson's for the unit-permeability parent; Wise's distinct contribution is the explicitly permeability-dependent extension.
