**Description.** Small-argument logarithmic reductions of the Saad–Gaba–Giroux buried-cable mutual and self earth-return approximations.

**Expression.** Source equations (31)–(32), with ``\gamma=0.57721\ldots`` the Euler constant.

```math
Z_m=\frac{\rho m^2}{2\pi}
\left[-\left\{\ln\!\left(\frac{md}{2}\right)+\gamma\right\}
+0.5-0.5m\ell\right],
\qquad\text{(31)}
```

```math
\begin{aligned}
Z_s&=\frac{\rho m^2}{2\pi}
\left[-\left\{\ln\!\left(\frac{mR}{2}\right)+\gamma\right\}
+0.5-mh\right], \\
m&=\sqrt{\frac{j\omega\mu}{\rho}} \\
d&=\sqrt{x^2+(h_i-h_j)^2} \\
\ell&=h_i+h_j.
\end{aligned}\qquad\text{(32)}
```

**Approximation.** Further approximation of source (26)–(27). It substitutes the first small-argument term ``K_0(md)=-\{\ln(md/2)+\gamma\}``, expands ``e^{-m\ell}\simeq1-m\ell``, and treats ``m^2x^2\ll4``; analogous substitutions give the self formula. Retained order is first order in the displayed exponent arguments, with higher Bessel and exponential terms discarded.

**Limitations.** This is narrower than the main Saad formula and must not be used as though (31)–(32) inherited the parent formula's wider tested range. ``\gamma`` here is Euler's constant, not a propagation or Fourier variable. The output remains only the earth-return component.

**Reference.** [Saad1996](@cite), equations (30)–(32), printed p. 1541; parent equations (26)–(27), printed p. 1539.

**Transcription source.** Original publication PDF. Equations (30)–(32), all coefficients ``0.5``, ``0.5m\ell`` and ``mh``, and the stated small-argument conditions were checked against rendered page images.

## Source transcription

The paper explicitly states the substitutions

```math
\begin{aligned}
K_0(md)&=-\left\{\ln\!\left(\frac{md}{2}\right)+\gamma\right\}, \\
e^{-m\ell}&\simeq1-m\ell,
\qquad |m^2x^2|<0.0625\ll4.
\end{aligned}\qquad\text{(30)}
```
