# Xue et al. quasi-TEM overhead MoM–SO Green function

| Field | Value |

| Family | External impedance |

**Description.** Replaces the earlier uniform-current overhead MoM–SO earth Green function by an air-propagation quasi-TEM prescription, converting unstable Bessel differences to a direct/image logarithm plus a convergent earth integral.

**Assumptions.**

| Field | Treatment | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impressed longitudinal propagation constant ``Γ`` | Quasi-TEM choice ``k_v=k_a`` for every mode. | Stated — before (16). |
| Air propagation constant ``γ_air`` | ``k_a^2=-j\omega\mu_0j\omega\varepsilon_0``. | Stated — (7). |
| Earth propagation constant ``γ_earth`` | ``k_e^2=-j\omega\mu_1(\sigma_1+j\omega\varepsilon_1)``. | Stated — (7). |
| Earth permittivity and displacement current | Retained in both air and earth. | Stated — (7). |
| Range of validity | Paper states MoM–SO quasi-TEM applicability up to 10 MHz; numerical stability is demonstrated above hundreds of kHz. | Stated — end of §I, §III. |
| Earth permeability ``μ_earth`` | Derivation sets ``\mu_1=\mu_0``. | Stated — before (8). |
| Arrangement | Multiphase overhead conductors of arbitrary heights and lateral positions. | Stated — Fig. 1 and §II. |
| Earth structure | Homogeneous earth half-space below air. | Stated — Fig. 1. |
| Conductor and insulation geometry | Ideal round overhead conductors; conductor skin/proximity handled separately by MoM–SO. | Stated — §II. |
| Constitutive and field assumptions | Linear homogeneous isotropic half-spaces, quasi-TEM reduction; shunt-admittance term omitted from the series Green function. | Stated — before (16). |
| Conventions | Source coordinates use ``x`` along the line and ``y,z`` transversely; ``D,d`` are image/direct distances. | Stated — Fig. 1, (18)–(19). |

**Expression.**

```math
G_{x0}^{E\prime}=-\frac{j\omega\mu_0}{2\pi}\mathbf\Lambda'\mathbf I,
\qquad\text{(16)}
```

where element ``m`` is

```math
\Lambda'_{1m}=\ln\frac{D(m)}{d(m)}+2\Lambda'_2(m),
\qquad\text{(17)}
```

```math
\begin{aligned}
D(m)&=\sqrt{(y+y_m)^2+(z-z_m)^2} \\
d(m)&=\sqrt{(y-y_m)^2+(z-z_m)^2},
\end{aligned}\qquad\text{(18,19)}
```

```math
\Lambda'_2(m)=\int_0^{\infty}
\frac{e^{-\lambda(y+y_m)}}{\lambda+\sqrt{\lambda^2+k_a^2-k_e^2}}
\cos[\lambda(z-z_m)]\,d\lambda.
\qquad\text{(20)}
```

**Approximation.** The full modal Green function (8) depends on unknown modal ``k_v`` and transformation matrix ``B``. The proposed phase form imposes ``k_v=k_a`` and drops the term assigned to shunt admittance, giving (16)–(20).

**Limitations.** Homogeneous nonmagnetic earth, quasi-TEM frequencies, ideal conductor geometry in this Green component, and series impedance only. It is not the four-layer or arbitrary-layer recursion later published by Xue et al.

**Reference.** [Xue2018a](@cite), equations (1)–(24), especially (16)–(20), PDF p. 2.

**Transcription source.** Original PSCC page image. Log ratio, factor 2, direct/image distances, exponent, denominator root and cosine argument were visually verified.

## Source transcription

The uniform-current comparator (21) uses a difference of two ``K_0`` functions and is identified by the paper as the source of high-frequency numerical instability. It is not silently substituted into this record.

## Evidence and approximation sources

The paper explicitly presents (17) as the modified Green function that removes the original MoM–SO instability. Later LCP software is validation context, not equation authority.
