**Description.** Four-layer Hertzian-potential spectral integral for overhead self/mutual earth-return impedance, retaining each layer's conductivity, permittivity, permeability and the air-referenced longitudinal prescription.

**Assumptions.**

**Expression.**

```math
Z_{eij}=\frac{j\omega\mu_0}{2\pi}
\left[\ln\left(\frac{D_2}{D_1}\right)
+\int_0^{+\infty}F(s)e^{-s(h_i+h_j)}\cos(sy_{ij})\,ds\right],
\qquad\text{(1)}
```

```math
\begin{aligned}
D_1&=\sqrt{y_{ij}^2+(h_i-h_j)^2} \\
D_2&=\sqrt{y_{ij}^2+(h_i+h_j)^2},
\end{aligned}\qquad\text{(2)}
```

```math
F(s)=\frac{A_1(T_5-T_1)+A_2(T_6-T_3)}{A_1T_2+A_2T_4}.
\qquad\text{(3)}
```

The layer roots are

```math
\begin{aligned}
a_n&=\sqrt{s^2+\gamma_n^2-\gamma_0^2} \\
\gamma_0^2&=j\omega\mu_0j\omega\varepsilon_0 \\
\gamma_n^2&=j\omega\mu_n(\sigma_n+j\omega\varepsilon_n).
\end{aligned}\qquad\text{(A.14,A.15)}
```

**Approximation.** No fitted approximation is applied to (1); ``F`` is the exact four-layer interface factor within the source's line model. Its explicit algebraic dependencies ``A_1,A_2,T_1,\ldots,T_6`` are printed in (A.1)–(A.15).

**Limitations.** The closed printed ``F`` is four-layer-specific and lengthy; extension to arbitrary ``N`` is supplied only for the EHEM and Green recursion, not this same ``F`` notation. Lateral inhomogeneity, finite length and higher conductor modes are absent. Full appendix dependencies are source-required.

**Reference.** [Xue2021](@cite), equations (1)–(3), (A.1)–(A.15), PDF pp. 2, 6–7.

**Transcription source.** Accepted IEEE page images. Integral bounds, logarithm, exponential, cosine, ``F`` numerator/denominator, root subtraction and constitutive constants were visually verified.

## Source transcription

For example, the terminal layer factors begin with ``T_1=P_{23}\Delta_1+Q_{23}\Delta_3``, ``T_2=P_{23}\Delta_2+Q_{23}\Delta_4``, ``T_3=W_{23}\Delta_1+V_{23}\Delta_3`` and ``T_4=W_{23}\Delta_2+V_{23}\Delta_4`` in (A.1)–(A.2). The remaining nested ``\Delta`` definitions are not silently simplified.

## Numerical evaluation

The final publication prints the magnetic appendix as (10)–(24)
and the electric appendix as (25)–(44), on pp. 3841–3843.
The following elimination of those interface equations avoids growing
exponentials without changing the four-layer field model.
Here ``\lambda`` denotes the source's integration variable ``s``,
``g_n=\gamma_n^2``,
``a_n=\sqrt{\lambda^2+g_n-g_0}``, and
``\delta_n=d_n-d_{n-1}``, with ``d_0=0``.
The source depths ``d_n`` are positive cumulative distances.

Define the layer input and transmission functions

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal U(a,w,\delta,b)&=
aw\frac{b+aw\tanh(a\delta)}{aw+b\tanh(a\delta)},\\
\mathcal T(a,w,\delta,b)&=
\frac{2aw e^{-a\delta}}{aw+b+(aw-b)e^{-2a\delta}}.
\end{aligned}
```

At ``a=0``, their continuous limits are
``\mathcal U=b/(1+b\delta/w)`` and
``\mathcal T=1/(1+b\delta/w)``.

Starting from ``B_4^{m}=a_4/\mu_4``, calculate upwards for
``n=3,2,1``:

```math
\begin{aligned}
B_n^{m}&=\mathcal U(a_n,1/\mu_n,\delta_n,B_{n+1}^{m}),\\
F&=\frac{2}{\lambda+\mu_0B_1^{m}}.
\end{aligned}
```

This ``F`` equals source equation (3). It is also the
permeability-weighted magnetic response used by the existing layered
impedance calculation, evaluated with the source's air propagation
reference. No second magnetic boundary solver is required.

Only the stated four-layer overhead model is selected here, not an
arbitrary-layer admittance extension or the EHEM approximation.
Self coefficients use ``D_1=r_i``, zero lateral separation in the
image/integral terms, and ``D_2=2h_i``.
The layer integrals are evaluated adaptively. Potential coefficients
are assembled before the matrix inverse.

## Limitations and discrepancies

The numerical form above is checked against the final publication's complete appendix, including unequal layer permeabilities. It does not broaden the source's four-layer, thin-wire, air-referenced transmission-line assumptions.
