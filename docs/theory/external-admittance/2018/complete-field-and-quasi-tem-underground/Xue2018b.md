**Expression.** qTEM earth-return potential coefficient and source matrix conversion, equations (34), (38)–(43), printed p. 2657.

```math
P_{e(nm)}=\frac{j\omega}{2\pi(\sigma_1+j\omega\varepsilon_1)}
\left[K_0(jk_ed_{nm})-K_0(jk_eD_{nm})
+2\Delta_5^{QT}-2k_e^2\Delta_6^{QT}\right],
\qquad\text{(34)}
```

```math
\Delta_5^{QT}=\int_0^{+\infty}
\left[\frac{e^{(h_n+h_m)u_1}}{u_0+k_a^2k_e^{-2}u_1}\right]
\frac{\lambda^2}{u_1^2}\cos[\lambda(d_n-d_m)]\,d\lambda,
\qquad\text{(38)}
```

```math
\Delta_6^{QT}=\int_0^{+\infty}
\left[\frac{e^{(h_n+h_m)u_1}}{u_0+u_1}\right]
\frac1{u_1^2}\cos[\lambda(d_n-d_m)]\,d\lambda.
\qquad\text{(39)}
```

After assembling coefficients,

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf P&=\mathbf P_i+\mathbf P_e \\
\mathbf Y&=j\omega\mathbf P^{-1}.
\end{aligned}\qquad\text{(42--43)}
```

The distances and roots are equations (35)–(36) and (40), transcribed in the companion impedance record. No element of ``Y`` is formed by reciprocating a single ``P_{e(nm)}``.

**Approximation.** Equation (34) is obtained from the complete-field admittance by setting ``k_v=0``; no integral is approximated further. The full-field formula retains modal ``k_v`` and the infinite-depth voltage reference.

**Limitations.** qTEM limits are as stated above. The positive depth exponent follows the source's negative-``z`` cable convention and is not changed. The source's total admittance requires adding ``P_i`` before inversion; ``P_e^{-1}`` alone is not the stated cable admittance. Modal root and square-root branch selection remain unstated beside the equations.

**Reference.** [Xue2018b](@cite), equations (14)–(19), (20)–(22), (29)–(34), and (38)–(43), printed pp. 2655–2657 (PDF pages 2–4).

**Transcription source.** Original IEEE page images. The source normalization, ``k_a^2k_e^{-2}`` denominator, ``\lambda^2/u_1^2`` and ``1/u_1^2`` factors, positive exponents and complete matrix inversion were visually verified.

## Source transcription

The complete-field relation is

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf Y_e&=(\mathbf Y_f\mathbf B^{-1})^{-1} \\
Y_{f(nv)}&=\sum_{m=1}^N Y_{fn}(d_n,h_n-r_{on},d_m,h_m,v)B_{mv},
\end{aligned}\qquad\text{(29--30)}
```

```math
Y_{fn}=\frac1{2\pi(\sigma_1+j\omega\varepsilon_1)}
\left[\frac{\Delta_1}{2}+\Delta_5-k_e^2\Delta_6+k_v^2\Delta_7\right],
\qquad\text{(31)}
```

```math
\Delta_5=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}
\left[\frac{e^{(z+h_m)u_{1v}}}{u_{0v}+k_a^2k_e^{-2}u_{1v}}\right]
\frac{\lambda^2}{u_{1v}^2}e^{-j\lambda(y-d_m)}\,d\lambda.
\qquad\text{(32)}
```

The shared ``\Delta_1,\Delta_6,\Delta_7`` definitions are reproduced in the companion impedance record. The complete-field phase-domain quantity is an actual admittance matrix by (29), whereas the qTEM scalar formula (34) is explicitly ``P_e`` and requires (42)–(43).

## Evidence and approximation sources

Equations (14)–(19) define the prior modal admittance. Equations (20)–(22) select the infinite-depth voltage reference. Equations (29)–(32) give the complete-field matrix, while (34), (38)–(40) give qTEM coefficients and (42)–(43) supply the assembly-to-admittance operation.

## Limitations and discrepancies

- This record deliberately distinguishes the complete-field matrix ``Y_e`` from qTEM coefficient entries ``P_e``.

## Numerical interpretation

The implementation retains the source's quasi-TEM propagation prescription. The series kernel combines S₁₁ and γ₁²S₁₃ algebraically before quadrature. For potential coefficients, the infinite, ground-surface, and penetration-depth voltage references remain explicit alternatives. The ground-surface form shares the Magalhães potential evaluator, and the penetration depth is evaluated as the reciprocal of the positive attenuation constant. The complete potential matrix is assembled before inversion.
