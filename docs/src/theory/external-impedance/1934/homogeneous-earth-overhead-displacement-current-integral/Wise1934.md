**Expression.** On printed p. 526, Wise reduces the mutual impedance to

```math
\begin{aligned}
Z_{12}
&=j2\omega\log\frac{\rho''}{\rho'}
+\frac{4\omega}{j(k_2^2-k^2)}
\int_0^\infty
\left(\sqrt{l^2+j(k_2^2-k^2)}-l\right)
e^{-wl}\cos(yl)\,dl \\
&=j2\omega\log\frac{\rho''}{\rho'}+4\omega(P+jQ).
\end{aligned}
```

With

```math
j(k_2^2-k^2)=4\pi\omega\sigma+j(\varepsilon-1)k^2
=4\pi\omega\sigma\left(1+j\frac{\varepsilon-1}{2c\lambda\sigma}\right)
=\alpha s^2,
```

the normalized correction printed immediately below is

```math
P+jQ=\frac{1}{s^2}\int_0^\infty
\left(\sqrt{v^2+js^2}-v\right)e^{-w'v}\cos(y'v)\,dv,
```

where ``\sqrt{\alpha}=\sqrt{4\pi\omega\sigma}``, ``w'=w\sqrt{\alpha}``, ``y'=y\sqrt{\alpha}``, and ``l=v\sqrt{\alpha}`` as printed.

**Approximation.** The earth-polarization integral is not approximated after the source fixes ``\gamma=jk`` and unit permeability. Wise then states that Carson's asymptotic and convergent series are reused by replacing Carson's ``r`` by ``r\sqrt{1+j(\varepsilon-1)/(2c\lambda\sigma)}``. Those evaluator series are not reproduced as separate physical formulations here.

**Limitations.** The imposed air-speed, zero-attenuation longitudinal wave is explicitly a first approximation. The formulation retains earth polarization but fixes relative permeability to unity and uses 1934 electromagnetic units. This transcription retains the original constants and treats later full-wave formulations separately.

**Reference.** [Wise1934](@cite).  Wise, “Propagation of High-Frequency Currents in Ground Return Circuits,” *Proceedings of the IRE* 22(4), 1934, pp. 522–527.

**Transcription source.** Original publication page image, printed p. 526 (PDF p. 5). The ``4\omega/[j(k_2^2-k^2)]`` prefactor, radical subtraction, exponent, cosine, normalized ``1/s^2`` factor, and constitutive definition were visually checked.

## Source transcription

The expressions above retain Wise's source order. The paper first integrates a horizontal-current-element wave function along an infinite wire, imposes ``\gamma=jk``, and only then reduces the spectral integral. Its statement that Carson's series can be modified by one complex scaling is a prescription for evaluating this integral. It does not separately claim physical priority.

## Notation map

### Numerical interpretation

The executable SI form uses lossless air and nonmagnetic earth, as in the
source, with ``\beta^2=j\omega\mu_0\sigma_g+
(j\omega)^2\mu_0(\varepsilon_g-\varepsilon_a)``. Rationalizing
``[\sqrt{u^2+\beta^2}-u]/\beta^2`` gives the bounded denominator
``[u+\sqrt{u^2+\beta^2}]^{-1}``. The SI correction multiplier is
``j\omega\mu_0/\pi``. The geometrical multiplier is
``j\omega\mu_0/(2\pi)``.

Self assembly sets the lateral separation to zero in the correction and the
wire radius only in the ideal-ground logarithm. The numerical integration
is checked against the independent difference-of-roots expression.
The original electromagnetic-unit equations above remain unchanged.

## Evidence and approximation sources

Wise explicitly lists the three Carson restrictions and removes only the neglected-polarization restriction. The proposed complex rescaling follows algebraically from the p. 526 normalized integral. Its retained and discarded-series order is whatever Carson's selected evaluator uses and is not generalized here.
