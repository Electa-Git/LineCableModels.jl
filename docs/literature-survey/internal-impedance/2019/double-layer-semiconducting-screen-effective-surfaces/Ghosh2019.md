**Description.** Cascades the exact inner/outer/mutual impedances of two bonded tubular media to obtain effective surface impedances of core–semiconductor and sheath–semiconductor assemblies.

**Assumptions.**

| Field | Treatment | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Air propagation constant ``γ_air`` | Not applicable to the bonded internal layers. | Scope. |

**Expression.** For two bonded tubular layers 1 and 2,

```math
Z_{22}=z_{2out}-\frac{z_{2m}^2}{z_{1out}+z_{2in}},\qquad
Z_{11}=z_{1in}-\frac{z_{1m}^2}{z_{1out}+z_{2in}},
\qquad\text{(11,16)}
```

```math
Z_{12}=\frac{z_{1m}z_{2m}}{z_{1out}+z_{2in}}.
\qquad\text{(17)}
```

For the sheath plus its inner semiconducting screen,

```math
Z_{sh,out}^{sem2}=z_{sh,out}-\frac{z_{sh,m}^2}{z_{sem2,out}+z_{sh,in}},\quad
Z_{sh,in}^{sem2}=z_{sem2,in}-\frac{z_{sem2,m}^2}{z_{sem2,out}+z_{sh,in}},
\qquad\text{(19,20)}
```

```math
Z_{sh,m}^{sem2}=\frac{z_{sem2,m}z_{sh,m}}{z_{sem2,out}+z_{sh,in}}.
\qquad\text{(21)}
```

**Approximation.** The reduction itself is algebraic once each homogeneous tubular component is evaluated. It inherits the concentric, longitudinal and material assumptions of the tubular Bessel parent equations (3)–(5).

**Limitations.** Concentric bonded layers only; no eccentricity or proximity between different cables. External earth and insulation field terms are inherited rather than derived. Interfaces must have no intervening insulation.

**Reference.** [Ghosh2019](@cite).  Ghosh, Ghosh, and Das, *IEEE Access* 7, 2019, DOI `10.1109/ACCESS.2019.2955026`, equations (1)–(34), especially (11), (16)–(21), printed pp. 169374–169376.

**Transcription source.** Original IEEE Access page images. Squared mutual terms, denominator ordering and unsquared cross-product in the mutual expression were visually verified.

## Source transcription

The component terms are generated by the modified-Bessel tubular equations (3)–(5). The cable mesh and phase transformations are printed in (22)–(34), but the new physical formula is the bonded-layer surface reduction above.

## Evidence and approximation sources

The paper attributes the single-annulus parent to Schelkunoff/Ametani and presents the bonded double-layer reduction and multiple-screen assembly as its explicit extension.

## Limitations and discrepancies

- The source says its effective model was developed earlier in reference [23]; this 2019 paper is an original accessible restatement/extension to multiple screens, not necessarily the priority source for every double-layer equation.
- Several PDF glyphs render as ``ʒ``/``ÿ`` in extracted text; equations were taken from page images.
