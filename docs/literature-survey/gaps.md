# Formula implementation gaps

A source issue prevents implementation only when the available equations do not determine the requested output, its material and geometric inputs, or a defensible numerical convention. Unavailable historical predecessors, damaged text conversion, and absent global error bounds do not prevent implementation when the publication supplies an evaluable formula.

No formula-bearing record in the present corpus is blocked under this criterion. Several records require an explicit token choice, a restricted output, or evaluation of a final equation without reproducing a defective intermediate derivation. The surveyed literature has one remaining case without an analytical formula, for which a numerical alternative is available.

## Remaining coverage limits

| Requested case | Numerical decision | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P.u.l. shunt coupling between overhead and underground conductors when the earth contains an arbitrary number of horizontal layers | Use the electric-potential FEM extraction in `:Mouhaidali2018` for arbitrary meshed media. Use `:MartinsBritto2024` or `:Pawlik2018` when the earth is homogeneous. | A working numerical shunt matrix is available. The surveyed literature does not provide a closed-form or spectral formula for this arbitrary-layer mixed geometry. |

Insulation impedance requires no additional dielectric-loss expression. Dielectric conduction and polarization contribute to shunt admittance; the represented magnetic-region terms supply the series insulation contribution.

## Numerical conventions

- Select ``\sqrt{z}`` so that the transverse field decays away from the interface: ``\Re\sqrt{z}\ge0``; continue from positive conductivity when the real part is zero.
- Evaluate improper spectral integrals by adaptive quadrature and expose absolute and relative tolerances as numerical inputs.
- Truncate infinite harmonic or Bessel series from an omitted-tail estimate or successive-term criterion, not from a fixed undocumented term count.
- Assemble potential or impedance matrices before inversion or Kron reduction. Do not replace a matrix inverse with entrywise reciprocals.
- Test homogeneous-layer limits, equal-material limits, reciprocity, matrix symmetry where the source model is reciprocal, low-frequency limits, and dimensional consistency.
- Associate each evaluator with the citation shown above. When an implementation corrects a printed token, identify the source equation and the implemented form in the formula documentation.

## Result

No reported source inconsistency prevents a working numerical implementation at the formula's supported geometry and material scope. Sector-shaped internal impedance now has an attributable analytical approximation, with numerical formulations retained for proximity-aware or shape-resolved calculations. The only remaining case without an analytical formula is p.u.l. mixed overhead/underground shunt coupling through an arbitrary number of horizontal earth layers; the corpus provides an attributable numerical formulation for that case.
