**Description.** Logarithmic self and mutual magnetic-field inductance contributions of offset round cores inside a cylindrical shield. The reference is the shield. The paper calls this field-region contribution external inductance relative to the metal conductors; it is inside the shield and is not earth-return impedance.

**Expression.** Self inductance, the first relation of (8), p. 1647:

```math
L_{ii}=\frac{\mu_0}{2\pi}
\mathop{\mathrm{Ln}}\left[\frac{c_1^2-b_i^2}{c_1a_i}\right].
\qquad\text{(8, inductance relation)}
```

Mutual inductance, (15), p. 1648:

```math
L_{ji}=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}
\mathop{\mathrm{Ln}}\left[
\frac{c_1^4+(b_ib_j)^2-2(b_ib_j)c_1^2\mathop{\mathrm{Cos}}(\theta_{i,j})}
{c_1^2[b_i^2+b_j^2-2b_ib_j\mathop{\mathrm{Cos}}(\theta_{i,j})]}
\right].\qquad\text{(15)}
```

The source itself supplies the inductive use of these geometric expressions: the self impedance in (11) contains the additive term

```math
+j\omega\frac{\mu_0}{2\pi}
\mathop{\mathrm{Ln}}\left[\frac{c_1^2-b_i^2}{c_1a_i}\right],
\qquad\text{field-region term of (11)},
```

and the mutual relation in (14), whose left side is ``(R_{j,i}+jL_{j,i}\omega)I_i``, contains

```math
+j\omega\frac{\mu_0I_i}{4\pi}
\mathop{\mathrm{Ln}}\left[
\frac{c_1^4+(b_ib_j)^2-2(b_ib_j)c_1^2\mathop{\mathrm{Cos}}(\theta_{i,j})}
{c_1^2[b_i^2+b_j^2-2b_ib_j\mathop{\mathrm{Cos}}(\theta_{i,j})]}
\right],\qquad\text{field-region term of (14)}.
```

The factor ``I_i`` belongs to the original mutual voltage-gradient relation and is not removed here to manufacture a newly named impedance. ``\mu_0`` is the source's reference permeability; ``a_i,b_i,b_j,c_1`` are lengths, ``\theta_{i,j}`` is the angular separation of the two axis-offset vectors, and ``\omega`` is angular frequency. The logarithm arguments are as printed; no absolute value, alternative image distance, or finite-core correction is inserted.

**Approximation.** Published lossless field-region expressions within the filament-based construction. The author calls the mutual inductance classical and obtains it from the filament mutual expression; it is not claimed here as an original 1995 discovery or an exact arbitrary-finite-core solution. No explicit expansion parameter, truncation order, discarded geometric terms, or error bound is given.

**Limitations.** These formulas do not include core skin effect, other-core eddy currents, finite-shield metal impedance, earth return, dielectric loss, or a terminal-network transformation. No author-provided self-substitution of (15) is given: the self expression (8) is retained independently.

**Reference.** [Kane1995](@cite).  Kane, Ahmad and Auriol (1995), original pp. 1646–1648, Fig. 1, equations (8),(15), and the field-region terms of (4),(7),(11),(14).

**Transcription source.** Original IEEE article page images inspected at PDF pages 2–4. Self and mutual factors, the squared-distance denominator, and the unrooted mutual logarithm argument were checked visually. No equation was reconstructed from PDF text or a later manuscript.

## Source transcription

The geometric equations in the formula section are in unchanged original notation. Their full source order and use are:

1. Equation (4), p. 1647, contains ``+j\omega\mu_0I_i/(2\pi)`` times the self logarithm as its third additive term; the prose explicitly identifies this term as the external inductance between the core and shield.
2. Equation (7) removes that field-region term to isolate the shield surface contribution:

```math
(R_s+jL_s\omega)=(R_g+jL_g\omega)
-j\omega\frac{\mu_0}{2\pi}
\mathop{\mathrm{Ln}}\left[\frac{c_1^2-b_i^2}{c_1a_i}\right].\qquad\text{(7)}
```

3. Equation (8) prints ``L_{ii}`` exactly as displayed above, followed by the separate capacitance product retained in the [dielectric relation record](../../../insulation-admittance/1995/offset-cores-shield-lossless-capacitance-potential-relations/Kane1995.md).
4. Equation (11) places the positive self field-region impedance term among shield and solid-core contributions. Equation (14) places the positive mutual field-region term among the corresponding filament–shield terms, with its original current factor. Both complete impedance expressions are retained in the [finite-shield model record](../../../internal-impedance/1995/finite-shield-core-loop-skin-and-proximity-series/Kane1995.md).
5. Equation (15) identifies ``L_{ji}`` as displayed above; equation (16) then supplies a dielectric potential-coefficient relation, not an impedance amendment.

Only (7) is a source-provided subtraction. No additional subtraction, matrix inversion, image-conductor reparametrization, or terminal reference change is introduced.

## Evidence and approximation sources

- Geometry and reference definition are supplied by section II/Fig. 1. The source explicitly begins the self and mutual constructions with filamentary cores; it later adds metal skin/proximity effects separately.
- The three-term interpretation below (4) and subtraction (7) establish that the logarithm is a field-region contribution, not the total metal surface impedance. This evidence controls the family classification despite the paper's use of the word external.
- Equation (15) is labelled classical by the source. The record preserves a distinct published offset-core geometry and its assembly, without assigning historical priority from that adjective or the 1995 publication date.
- Section VI's four-core example at 10 MHz compares inductance matrices with values based on measured capacitances; it reports approximately 10% maximum relative difference for that example. It does not state a general geometric accuracy guarantee for (8),(15).

## Limitations and discrepancies

- **Physical model restriction:** these are the source's filament-based/lossless field-region formulas, not a solved finite-radius multiple-core boundary problem with all proximity effects.
- **No automatic self limit:** the mutual expression is for distinct cores. Its distance denominator is not used to declare the source's separate self expression defective or to reconstruct a regularized self formula.
- **No unsupported constitutive generalization:** ``\mu_0`` stays fixed; no independent insulation permeability, complex soil parameter, or displacement-current addition is introduced.
- **Conversion status:** no corresponding original-paper Markdown was located; all displayed expressions follow the original page images.
