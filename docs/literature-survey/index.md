## Screening status

The formula records were checked against an original publication, original thesis, original-author manuscript or report, reproduction, or corrected author edition wherever available. Markdown and OCR conversions were used for navigation, never as authority where a page image was available.

The second pass searched all 4,066 Zotero attachments. Metadata, filenames, and six full-text query clusters produced 1,568 positive attachments grouped under 1,211 parent candidates. The remaining candidates selected by attachment score or title were opened at their equation pages, and every positive parent was added to `sources.tsv` with a terminal disposition. A subsequent PDF reconciliation screened the newly available sources and corrected the earlier title-based classifications.

“Extracted” is publication-level and may link several family records. “Duplicate” means an earlier or later witness, specialization, or algebraically repeated physical formulation whose attribution is retained in a linked record; it does not mean that the publication was ignored. “Application-only” means the paper uses existing physical parameter equations or contributes simulation, fitting, field-response, or modal analysis without a distinct requested-family formula. Completion is relative to the accessible inventoried collection, not to all published literature.
