**Description.** Closed-form self and mutual earth-return impedances for parallel horizontal cables buried in a homogeneous semi-infinite earth. The formula approximates the interface integral in the Pollaczek/Wedepohl representation while retaining its direct modified-Bessel term.

**Expression.** Proposed mutual and self impedances, source equations (5)–(6), repeated after the derivation as (26)–(27).

```math
Z_m=\frac{\rho m^2}{2\pi}
\left\{K_0(md)+\frac{2}{4+m^2x^2}e^{-\ell m}\right\},
\qquad \ell=h_i+h_j,
\tag{5,26}

Z_s=\frac{\rho m^2}{2\pi}
\left\{K_0(mR)+\frac{2}{4+m^2R^2}e^{-2hm}\right\},
\tag{6,27}

m=\sqrt{\frac{j\omega\mu}{\rho}},
\qquad
d=\sqrt{x^2+(h_i-h_j)^2},
\qquad
D=\sqrt{x^2+(h_i+h_j)^2}.
```

``K_0`` is the modified Bessel function of the second kind. ``Z_m`` is the mutual earth-return impedance; ``Z_s`` is obtained from the mutual geometry by ``x=R`` and ``h_i=h_j=h``. The displayed approximation eliminates the separate ``K_0(mD)`` term appearing in the parent expression because it is cancelled by the approximated interface integral.

**Approximation.** Starting from the Pollaczek/Wedepohl expressions (1)–(4), the authors deform the integration path, approximate ``\sqrt{\delta^2+1}/(\delta+\sqrt{\delta^2+1})`` by ``(1+e^{-2\delta})/2`` in (15), and then use ``\sqrt{\delta^2+1}\simeq1`` in the rapidly decaying part, equations (20)–(21). The paper reports about 3% maximum relative error for the first kernel approximation and restricts the contour proof to ``x/\ell<1``. A further small-argument reduction is kept in a separate record.

**Limitations.** Homogeneous nonmagnetic earth, parallel buried conductors, neglected displacement current, filamentary earth-return treatment, and long wavelength. The result is only the earth-return impedance component. The source does not state the square-root branch of ``m`` separately from the printed radical.

**Reference.** [Saad1996](@cite), equations (1)–(6), printed pp. 1536–1537; equations (7)–(27), printed pp. 1537–1539; error discussion, printed p. 1540.

**Transcription source.** Original publication PDF. Equations (1)–(6), (15), (20)–(27), every exponential, Bessel argument, denominator, and geometry definition were checked against rendered page images. PDF text extraction was used only to locate sections.

## Source transcription

The parent mutual and self representations printed before approximation are

```math
Z_m=\frac{\rho m^2}{2\pi}
\left\{K_0(md)-K_0(mD)+J_m\right\},
\tag{1}

J_m=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\frac{e^{-(h_i+h_j)\sqrt{\gamma^2+m^2}}}
{|\gamma|+\sqrt{\gamma^2+m^2}}e^{j\gamma x}\,d\gamma,
\tag{2}

Z_s=\frac{\rho m^2}{2\pi}
\left\{K_0(mR)-K_0\!\left(m\sqrt{R^2+4h^2}\right)+J_s\right\},
\tag{3}

J_s=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\frac{e^{-2h\sqrt{\gamma^2+m^2}}}
{|\gamma|+\sqrt{\gamma^2+m^2}}e^{j\gamma R}\,d\gamma.
\tag{4}
```

These are retained as the source-declared parents of (5)–(6), not attributed to Saad et al. as original Pollaczek/Wedepohl kernels.

## Limitations and discrepancies

- The paper's reference list gives Pollaczek's 1931 French publication as the historical source even though the introduction also discusses the 1926 overhead result. This record does not collapse those bibliographic identities.
- The source's symbol ``\gamma`` in (2) is an integration variable; it must not be confused with ``m`` or with an impressed longitudinal ``Γ``.
- The Markdown conversion omits the equations; the equations were transcribed from page images.
