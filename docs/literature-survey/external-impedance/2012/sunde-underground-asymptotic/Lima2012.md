**Expression.** Proposed asymptotic impedance, source equation (16), printed p. 22.

```math
z_{ij}=\frac{j\omega\mu}{2\pi}\left[
K_0(\eta d)+\frac{\ell^2-x^2}{D^2}K_2(\eta D)
-2\frac{\ell^2-x^2}{\eta^2D^4}(1+\ell\eta)e^{-\ell\eta}
\right],
\qquad\text{(16)}
```

```math
\ell=h_i+h_j,\qquad
d=\sqrt{x^2+(h_i-h_j)^2},\qquad
D=\sqrt{x^2+\ell^2},\qquad
\eta=\sqrt{j\omega\mu_0(\sigma+j\omega\varepsilon)}.
```

For self impedance, ``x`` is replaced by outer insulation radius ``R`` and ``\ell=2h_i``.

**Approximation.** The source rewrites the parent residual ``J_m`` as (13)–(15), then expands the ``I_4`` integrand about ``\xi\to\infty`` and retains the leading terms to obtain (16). The stated expansion is asymptotic and divergent; no convergence order or remainder bound is supplied.

**Limitations.** Equation (16) is stated valid while ``\ell/2>x`` and is tested only to 2 MHz in the reported cable examples. It has no air bulk constant, earth stratification, internal impedance, finite-length effect, or nonparallel geometry. Root branches are unstated. The parent (11) visibly prints ``-K_1(\eta D)`` although the subsequent rearrangement contains ``K_0``/``K_2``; this suspected source-level notation conflict is not repaired.

**Reference.** [Lima2012](@cite), equations (11)–(17), printed p. 22 (PDF page 3).

**Transcription source.** Original publication page image. The ``K_0``/``K_2`` orders, all squared geometric factors, negative exponential term, self substitutions and explicit ``\ell/2>x`` condition were visually checked.

## Source transcription

The paper prints the parent and residual as

```math
z_{ij}=\frac{j\omega\mu}{2\pi}[K_0(\eta d)-K_1(\eta D)+2J_m],
\qquad\text{(11)}
```

```math
J_m=\int_0^\infty
\frac{e^{-\ell\sqrt{\xi^2+\eta^2}}}{\xi+\sqrt{\xi^2+\eta^2}}
\cos(x\xi)\,d\xi,
\qquad\text{(12)}
```

and equations (13)–(15) expose the ``I_4`` integral to which the asymptotic expansion is applied. The literal ``K_1`` in (11) is retained as a parent-source discrepancy.

## Evidence and approximation sources

The paper explicitly distinguishes its overhead exact transformation from this underground asymptotic approximation. Equations (11)–(15) are the recoverable parent; the paragraph before (16) states the expansion point and leading-term retention.

## Limitations and discrepancies

- Parent equation (11)'s ``K_1`` conflicts with the later algebra; no correction or inferred substitution is made.
- Published accuracy is empirical for the stated geometries and material models.
