**Description.** Per-unit-length self or mutual Carson ground-return impedance for parallel overhead conductors above a homogeneous earth, expressed exactly within the paper's quasi-TEM model through order-one Struve and Bessel functions.

**Expression.** Closed-form mutual impedance (7), printed p. 21.

```math
\begin{aligned}
z_{ij}={}&\frac{j\omega\mu_0}{4\eta\lambda_1}
\left[\mathbf H_1(\eta\lambda_1)-Y_1(\eta\lambda_1)-\frac{2}{\pi\eta\lambda_1}\right]\\
&+\frac{j\omega\mu_0}{4\eta\lambda_2}
\left[\mathbf H_1(\eta\lambda_2)-Y_1(\eta\lambda_2)-\frac{2}{\pi\eta\lambda_2}\right],
\end{aligned}
\qquad\text{(7)}
```

```math
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_1&=2h_i-jd_{ij} \\
\lambda_2&=2h_j+jd_{ij} \\
\eta&=\sqrt{j\omega\mu_0(\sigma+j\omega\varepsilon)}.
\end{aligned}
```

For self impedance the source sets horizontal separation to zero and ``\lambda_1=\lambda_2``; this evaluates the parent self integral (8).

**Approximation.** Not an analytical approximation of the parent Carson integral (1). The source applies an integral transformation and the integral definition of the Struve function to obtain (7). The physical quasi-TEM/Carson model remains restricted.

**Limitations.** Complex special-function evaluation can be numerically unstable in regimes reported on p. 22. The source does not state branch rules for ``\eta`` or the special functions beside (7). The expression does not contain internal impedance, finite radius, insulation, earth stratification, or nonparallel geometry.

**Reference.** [Lima2012](@cite), equations (1)–(8), printed p. 21 (PDF page 2).

**Transcription source.** Original publication page image. Both complex arguments, the two distinct height terms, the subtraction ``2/(\pi\eta\lambda_k)``, prefactors, and self statement were visually verified.

## Source transcription

The transformed expression is controlled by the source parent

```math
\begin{aligned}
z_{ij}&=\frac{j\omega\mu_0}{\pi}\int_0^\infty
\frac{e^{-\ell\xi}}{\xi+\sqrt{\xi^2+\eta^2}}\cos(d_{ij}\xi)\,d\xi \\
\ell&=h_i+h_j.
\end{aligned}\qquad\text{(1)}
```

Equations (2)–(6) split and transform this integral; they are derivation identities, not separate formulations.

## Evidence and approximation sources

The exact algebraic chain occupies (1)–(7), p. 21. Equation (8) and the sentence above it specify the self limit. Tests use several complex soil models but do not change the formula.

## Limitations and discrepancies

- This is exact only relative to the stated Carson/quasi-TEM parent; “actual solution” does not imply a full-wave model.
