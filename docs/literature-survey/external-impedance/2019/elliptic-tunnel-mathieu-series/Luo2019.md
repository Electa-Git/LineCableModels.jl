**Description.** Quasi-static boundary solution in elliptic cylindrical coordinates, using angular and modified radial Mathieu functions to express the reflected field and the resulting self/mutual tunnel earth-return impedance.

**Assumptions.**

| Field | Treatment | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Air propagation constant ``γ_air`` | Air/tunnel interior source field uses the low-frequency logarithmic vector potential. | Stated — §2 and (1). |

**Expression.** For source ``(\xi_1,\eta_1)`` and observation ``(\xi_2,\eta_2)``,

```math
Z_m=\frac{i\omega\mu_0}{2\pi}\left\{
\ln\!\frac{1}{c\sqrt{(\cosh\xi_2\cos\eta_2-\cosh\xi_1\cos\eta_1)^2
+(\sinh\xi_2\sin\eta_2-\sinh\xi_1\sin\eta_1)^2}}
+F_0+2\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{F_n\cosh(n\xi_2)\cos(n\eta_2)+G_n\sinh(n\xi_2)\sin(n\eta_2)}{n}
\right\}.
\qquad\text{(35)}
```

The self result is

```math
Z_s=\frac{i\omega\mu_0}{2\pi}\left\{
\ln\frac1{r_0}+F_0+2\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{F_n\cosh[n(\xi_1+\delta\xi_1)]\cos(n\eta_1)+G_n\sinh[n(\xi_1+\delta\xi_1)]\sin(n\eta_1)}{n}
\right\},
\qquad\text{(36)}
```

with ``\delta\xi_1=(r_0/c)(\cosh^2\xi_1-\cos^2\eta_1)^{-1/2}``.

**Approximation.** No fitted impedance approximation is used, but the source field is quasi-static and the infinite Mathieu/Fourier expansions require numerical truncation. Special major/minor-axis reductions are printed in (38)–(42).

**Limitations.** One elliptic cylindrical void, homogeneous infinite wall, thin conductors, no displacement current, and no finite-length/end effects. Numerical use requires the full coefficient systems (17)–(32) and the source's complex-argument sign rules (43)–(50).

**Reference.** [Luo2019](@cite), equations (1)–(52), especially (35)–(37), printed p. 4926.

**Transcription source.** Original IET page images. The leading logarithm, both hyperbolic/trigonometric series terms, self-coordinate increment and time-sign prefactor were visually verified.

## Source transcription

The reflected vector potential is expanded in (33), and ``E_z^{(s)}=-i\omega A_z^{(s)}`` in (34). Coefficients ``F_n,G_n`` are outputs of the boundary systems (25)–(31); they are not reconstructed or silently renormalized here.

## Evidence and approximation sources

The authors present the result as a generalization of circular-tunnel formulas. Exactness is confined to their quasi-static boundary-value model; it is not a full-wave result.
