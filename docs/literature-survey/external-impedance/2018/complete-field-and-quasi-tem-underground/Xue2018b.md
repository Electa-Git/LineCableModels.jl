**Expression.** Quasi-TEM self/mutual earth-return impedance, source equation (33), printed p. 2657.

```math
Z_{e(nm)}=\frac{j\omega\mu_0}{2\pi}
\left[K_0(jk_ed_{nm})-K_0(jk_eD_{nm})
+2\Delta_4^{QT}-2k_e^2\Delta_6^{QT}\right],
\qquad\text{(33)}
```

```math
d_{nm}=\sqrt{(h_n-h_m)^2+(d_n-d_m)^2},\qquad
D_{nm}=\sqrt{(h_n+h_m)^2+(d_n-d_m)^2},
\qquad\text{(35--36)}
```

```math
\Delta_4^{QT}=\int_0^{+\infty}
\left[\frac{e^{(h_n+h_m)u_1}}{u_0+u_1}\right]
\frac{\lambda^2}{u_1^2}\cos[\lambda(d_n-d_m)]\,d\lambda,
\qquad\text{(37)}
```

```math
\Delta_6^{QT}=\int_0^{+\infty}
\left[\frac{e^{(h_n+h_m)u_1}}{u_0+u_1}\right]
\frac{1}{u_1^2}\cos[\lambda(d_n-d_m)]\,d\lambda,
\qquad\text{(39)}
```

```math
u_0=\sqrt{\lambda^2-k_a^2},\qquad
u_1=\sqrt{\lambda^2-k_e^2},\qquad
k_a^2=\omega^2\mu_0\epsilon_0,qquad
k_e^2=-j\omega\mu_0(\sigma_1+j\omega\epsilon_1).
\qquad\text{(10,40)}
```

Because the cable ``z`` coordinates are negative, the literally positive source exponent ``(h_n+h_m)u_1`` represents decay under its coordinate convention; it is not silently changed to a minus sign.

**Approximation.** Equation (33) follows from the complete-field formula solely by setting each unknown modal ``k_v=0``. It is an exact spectral representation within that qTEM reduction, not a closed-form approximation of its integrals.

**Limitations.** qTEM is stated limited to 10 MHz and the wavelength/electrical-size conditions above. Complete-field evaluation requires solving the modal equation first and a voltage reference at infinite earth depth. Square-root decay branches and modal root selection are not stated beside the equations. This record retains the source's positive depth exponent exactly.

**Reference.** [Xue2018b](@cite), equations (1)–(18), (20)–(28), and (33), (35)–(40), printed pp. 2655–2657 (PDF pages 2–4).

**Transcription source.** Original IEEE page images. The signs of ``k_a^2,k_e^2``, positive depth exponents, ``\lambda^2/u_1^2`` and ``1/u_1^2`` factors, Bessel arguments, modal products and self prescription were visually checked.

## Source transcription

The complete-field phase-domain impedance is

```math
\mathbf Z_e=\mathbf Z_f\mathbf B^{-1},
\qquad
Z_{f(nv)}=\sum_{m=1}^N Z_{fn}(d_n,h_n-r_{on},d_m,h_m,v)B_{mv},
\qquad\text{(23--24)}
```

```math
Z_{fn}=\frac{j\omega\mu_0}{2\pi}
\left[\frac{\Delta_1}{2}+\Delta_4-k_e^2\Delta_6+k_v^2\Delta_7\right].
\qquad\text{(25)}
```

The source defines

```math
\Delta_1=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}
\left[e^{-|z-h_m|u_{1v}}-e^{(z+h_m)u_{1v}}\right]
\frac{e^{-j(y-d_m)\lambda}}{u_{1v}}\,d\lambda,
\qquad\text{(6)}
```

```math
\Delta_4=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}
\left[\frac{e^{(z+h_m)u_{1v}}}{u_{0v}+u_{1v}}\right]
\frac{\lambda^2}{u_{1v}^2}e^{-j\lambda(y-d_m)}\,d\lambda,
\qquad\text{(26)}
```

```math
\Delta_6=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}
\left[\frac{e^{(z+h_m)u_{1v}}}{u_{0v}+u_{1v}}\right]
\frac{1}{u_{1v}^2}e^{-j\lambda(y-d_m)}\,d\lambda,
\qquad\text{(27)}
```

```math
\Delta_7=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}
\left[\frac{e^{(z+h_m)u_{1v}}}{u_{0v}+k_a^2k_e^{-2}u_{1v}}\right]
\frac{1}{u_{1v}^2}e^{-j\lambda(y-d_m)}\,d\lambda.
\qquad\text{(28)}
```

## Evidence and approximation sources

Equations (14)–(18) establish the modal parent; (20)–(28) define complete-field voltage and impedance; (33) and (35)–(40) are the source's qTEM specialization. The 10 MHz/electrical-size statement follows immediately after (40).

## Limitations and discrepancies

- The source calls ``d_n`` a lateral coordinate while ``d_{nm}`` is a distance; both tokens are preserved.
