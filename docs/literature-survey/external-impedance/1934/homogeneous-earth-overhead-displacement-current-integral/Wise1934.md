**Description.** Mutual per-unit-length overhead ground-return impedance over a homogeneous earth when earth displacement/polarization current is retained under Wise's imposed air-speed longitudinal propagation.

**Expression.** On printed p. 526, Wise reduces the mutual impedance to

```math
\begin{aligned}
Z_{12}
&=j2\omega\log\frac{\rho''}{\rho'}
+\frac{4\omega}{j(k_2^2-k^2)}
\int_0^\infty
\left(\sqrt{l^2+j(k_2^2-k^2)}-l\right)
e^{-wl}\cos(yl)\,dl \\
&=j2\omega\log\frac{\rho''}{\rho'}+4\omega(P+jQ).
\end{aligned}
```

With

```math
j(k_2^2-k^2)=4\pi\omega\sigma+j(\varepsilon-1)k^2
=4\pi\omega\sigma\left(1+j\frac{\varepsilon-1}{2c\lambda\sigma}\right)
=\alpha s^2,
```

the normalized correction printed immediately below is

```math
P+jQ=\frac{1}{s^2}\int_0^\infty
\left(\sqrt{v^2+js^2}-v\right)e^{-w'v}\cos(y'v)\,dv,
```

where ``\sqrt{\alpha}=\sqrt{4\pi\omega\sigma}``, ``w'=w\sqrt{\alpha}``, ``y'=y\sqrt{\alpha}``, and ``l=v\sqrt{\alpha}`` as printed.

**Approximation.** The earth-polarization integral is not approximated after the source fixes ``\gamma=jk`` and unit permeability. Wise then states that Carson's asymptotic and convergent series are reused by replacing Carson's ``r`` by ``r\sqrt{1+j(\varepsilon-1)/(2c\lambda\sigma)}``; those evaluator series are not reproduced as separate physical formulations here.

**Limitations.** The imposed air-speed, zero-attenuation longitudinal wave is explicitly a first approximation. The formulation retains earth polarization but fixes relative permeability to unity and uses 1934 electromagnetic units. This record does not translate its constants to SI or merge it with later full-wave formulations.

**Reference.** [Wise1934](@cite).  Wise, “Propagation of High-Frequency Currents in Ground Return Circuits,” *Proceedings of the IRE* 22(4), 1934, pp. 522–527.

**Transcription source.** Original publication page image, printed p. 526 (PDF p. 5). The ``4\omega/[j(k_2^2-k^2)]`` prefactor, radical subtraction, exponent, cosine, normalized ``1/s^2`` factor, and constitutive definition were visually checked.

## Source transcription

The expressions above retain Wise's source order. The paper first integrates a horizontal-current-element wave function along an infinite wire, imposes ``\gamma=jk``, and only then reduces the spectral integral. Its statement that Carson's series can be modified by one complex scaling is an evaluator prescription dependent on this integral, not a separate claim of physical priority.

## Evidence and approximation sources

Wise explicitly lists the three Carson restrictions and removes only the neglected-polarization restriction. The proposed complex rescaling follows algebraically from the p. 526 normalized integral; its retained/discarded-series order is whatever Carson's selected evaluator uses and is not generalized here.

## Limitations and discrepancies

- Optical extraction confuses ``l``, ``1``, primes, and radicals; the page image controls this record.
- A source-provided capacitance integral is described but not evaluated on p. 525, so no external-admittance record is created from this paper.
