**Description.** Four-layer overhead potential-coefficient integral with a source-defined displacement-current correction factor, assembled and inverted as a matrix to obtain earth-return admittance.

**Assumptions.**

| Field | Treatment | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impressed longitudinal propagation constant ``Γ`` | Air-referenced axial prescription through ``a_n=\sqrt{s^2+\gamma_n^2-\gamma_0^2}``. | Stated — §II-A, (A.14). |
| Air propagation constant ``γ_air`` | ``\gamma_0^2=j\omega\mu_0j\omega\epsilon_0``. | Stated — (A.14). |
| Earth propagation constant ``γ_earth`` | Layer-specific ``\gamma_n^2=j\omega\mu_n(\sigma_n+j\omega\epsilon_n)``. | Stated — (A.15). |
| Earth permittivity and displacement current | Retained in the nested ``G`` factor and all bulk constants. | Stated — (5)–(6), Appendix A2. |
| Range of validity | Exact within the source field/TL model; source cautions about TL applicability at high frequency. | Stated — §§II, III-C. |

**Expression.**

```math
\mathbf Y_e=j\omega\mathbf P_e^{-1},
\qquad\text{(4)}
```

```math
P_{eij}=\frac{1}{2\pi\epsilon_0}
\left[\ln\left(\frac{D_2}{D_1}\right)
+\int_0^{+\infty}G(s)e^{-s(h_i+h_j)}\cos(sy_{ij})\,ds\right],
\qquad\text{(5)}
```

```math
G(s)=F(s)+\frac{T'_1+T'_2T'_4}{T'_3}.
\qquad\text{(6)}
```

Here ``F(s)`` is (3) of the companion impedance record; ``T'_1,\ldots,T'_4`` and their nested factors are printed in (A.16)–(A.35), using the same roots (A.14)–(A.15).

**Approximation.** No fitted approximation is applied to (5); it is the source's exact four-layer potential entry within the selected longitudinal/TL model.

**Limitations.** Matrix inversion is indispensable. The printed coefficient is four-layer-specific and its appendix dependency graph is extensive. No insulation or conductor-internal admittance is included. Lateral variation and finite length are absent.

**Reference.** [Xue2021](@cite), equations (2)–(6), (A.14)–(A.35), PDF pp. 2, 7–8.

**Transcription source.** Accepted IEEE page images. Matrix inverse, ``1/(2\pi\epsilon_0)``, logarithm, spectral factor and primed ``T`` combination were visually verified.

## Source transcription

The source explicitly calls the diagonal and off-diagonal ``P_{eij}`` entries the earth-return potential-coefficient matrix and then applies a single matrix inverse. Entrywise reciprocal interpretation is excluded.
