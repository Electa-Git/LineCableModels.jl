**Expression.** Source equations (9)–(10) and the two unnumbered coarser approximations on printed p. 371.

```math
\begin{aligned}
u^2&=j e^{j2\eta} \\
u&=e^{j(\eta+\pi/4)} \\
a&=\varepsilon-j2c\lambda\sigma=1/\tau^2, \\
g'&=(h+z-jy)\xi\sqrt{\alpha} \\
g''&=(h+z+jy)\xi\sqrt{\alpha} \\
g&=(h+z)\xi\sqrt{\alpha}, \\
r_1&=\frac{u}{2}\left(1-\sqrt{1-\frac{4}{a+1}}\right) \\
r_2&=\frac{u}{2}\left(1+\sqrt{1-\frac{4}{a+1}}\right), \\
M+jN&\approx
\frac{1}{2(a+1)(r_2-r_1)}
\left[
-r_2e^{g'r_1}\mathop{\mathrm{li}}(e^{-g'r_1})
+r_1e^{g'r_2}\mathop{\mathrm{li}}(e^{-g'r_2})
-r_2e^{g''r_1}\mathop{\mathrm{li}}(e^{-g''r_1})
+r_1e^{g''r_2}\mathop{\mathrm{li}}(e^{-g''r_2})
\right],
\end{aligned}\qquad\text{(9)}
```

```math
\begin{aligned}
M+jN&\approx
\frac{-r_2e^{gr_1}\mathop{\mathrm{li}}(e^{-gr_1})
      +r_1e^{gr_2}\mathop{\mathrm{li}}(e^{-gr_2})}
     {(a+1)(r_2-r_1)} \\
y&=0, \\
M+jN&\approx\int_0^\infty\frac{e^{-gt}}{u+at} \\
dt&=-\frac{1}{a}e^{gu/a}\mathop{\mathrm{li}}(e^{-gu/a}) \\
y&=0, \\
M+jN&\approx\int_0^1\frac{dt}{u+at}
+\int_1^\infty\frac{e^{-gt}}{(1+a)t} \\
dt&=\frac{1}{a}\log\left(1+\frac{a}{u}\right)
-\frac{1}{1+a}\mathop{\mathrm{li}}(e^{-g}) \\
y&=0,\quad g\text{ very small}.
\end{aligned}\qquad\text{(10)}
```

The source defines ``\mathop{\mathrm{li}}(e^{-z})=-\int_z^\infty e^{-t}/t\,dt`` and provides both a power series and an asymptotic expansion for its evaluation.

**Approximation.** The parent integral is [Wise's equation (8)](../homogeneous-earth-overhead-potential-coefficient/Wise1948.md). The source expands ``\sqrt{t^2+u^2}=t+u-tu/(t+u)+\cdots``, retains the displayed truncated expression, performs partial fractions with roots ``r_1,r_2``, and obtains (9). Equation (10) imposes ``y=0``. The final unnumbered form further assumes ``g`` is very small. The source gives no formal remainder bound; its reported sub-one-percent discrepancy applies only to checked points.

**Limitations.** Equations (10) and both coarser formulas do not cover nonzero transverse separation. The tested points and parameter bounds for the reported error are not tabulated. Complex square-root and logarithmic-integral branches are not fully specified beyond ``-\pi\leq\arg z\leq\pi`` for the displayed ``\mathop{\mathrm{li}}`` expansion.

**Reference.** [Wise1948](@cite), equations (9)–(10), printed p. 370; unnumbered coarser forms, printed p. 371.

**Transcription source.** Original publication PDF. Definitions, signs, four exponential-integral terms in (9), the ``y=0`` reduction, and both p. 371 integrals were checked against rendered page images.

## Source transcription

The operation printed immediately before (9) is

```math
\sqrt{t^2+u^2}=t+u-\frac{tu}{t+u}+\cdots,
```

followed by

```math
\frac{1}{\sqrt{t^2+u^2}+at}
\approx\frac{t+u}{(a+1)t^2+(a+1)tu+u^2}
=\frac{t+r_1+r_2}{(a+1)(t+r_1)(t+r_2)}.
```

This record keeps the source's ``\approx`` relation and does not upgrade it to equality.

- Parent integral and scaling variables: equation (8), printed pp. 365 and 369.

## Limitations and discrepancies

- The source says (9) is “fairly good” and reports under one percent discrepancy at the checked points but supplies neither a global domain nor a global error bound.
- The printed asymptotic remainder is denoted ``R_n`` without a bound in the inspected page.
- No equation-preserving Markdown was available; OCR text damages several radicals and exponential-integral terms.
